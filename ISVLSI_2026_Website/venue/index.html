The City of Joy – Kolkata

Kolkata, the cultural capital of India, is a vibrant metropolis that beautifully blends rich heritage with modern development. Located in the eastern part of the country, the city is known for its historic architecture, intellectual traditions, literature, art, and warm hospitality. Kolkata has long been a center for education, science, and innovation, hosting several renowned universities, research institutions, and technology organizations.

Visitors to the city can experience iconic landmarks, diverse cuisine, and a lively cultural atmosphere. With excellent connectivity through Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport and a well-developed urban infrastructure, Kolkata provides an ideal setting for international conferences, academic exchange, and collaborative research. The city’s unique charm and dynamic academic ecosystem make it a welcoming destination for participants from around the world.
